CHANGES FROM v1.1
------------------
Supersedes: v1.1-protocolos, which remains valid and unmodified.
The original pre-registration v1.0-protocolos-pre-registro (DOI
10.5281/zenodo.21904456) also remains valid and unmodified.
A0-v1 refined in four respects, all from external methodological
review (all in Sections 4 and 5 of the A0 protocol):
  (1) UNIFORM-POWER SIZING. N per orientation changed from
      primary-target sizing (444,444 / 3,111,111 / 4,444,444; total
      8,000,000) to uniform-power sizing (4,444,445 each; total
      13,333,335). Rounding convention: N_min is a lower bound,
      integer ceiling is used. Rationale: since all nine cells are
      evaluated, primary-target sizing would give orientation 1 only
      0.14x the N needed to test n=28 and 0.10x for n=40 -- "absence"
      there would be lack of power, not evidence of absence.
      Cost: +67% data (80s -> 133s at 100 kHz).
  (2) TWO-LEVEL DECISION HIERARCHY. Level 1 = primary criterion
      (necessary and sufficient for a positive): the three diagonal
      target cells. Level 2 = the six off-diagonal cells qualify the
      interpretation but are NOT a requirement for the positive.
      This resolves the earlier ambiguity between "all nine cells are
      confirmatory" and "the six negatives are reinforcement, not a
      hard requirement".
  (3) A0-NULL IS NOT PURE QM, made explicit. A0-null is an
      INTERMEDIATE hypothesis: D3 holds and modulation exists (m>0),
      but AII is not the mechanism selecting n_mod -- resonance would
      be determined by delta-theta alone, rotationally invariant.
      Pure QM (m=0) remains a distinct third hypothesis.
  (4) Hypothesis-table label adjusted from "A0-nula (ressonancia so
      via delta-theta, ignorando AII)" to "A0-nula (D3 vale, AII nao
      determina n_mod)".
All four changes tighten or clarify the criteria; none relaxes them.
CxB-v1 rules remain UNCHANGED from v1.0.

CxB-v1 DECISION RULE (locked)
------------------------------
Hierarchical -- NOT "any pair of {3,4,5}":
  - FULL confirmation of C: configs 3 AND 4 AND 5 exceed threshold.
  - PRIMARY (unreplicated) confirmation of C: 3 AND 4 exceed, 5 does
    not -- report as such; do NOT treat as equivalent to full
    confirmation.
  - INCONSISTENT-WITH-C pattern: only one of {3,4}, or 5 alone --
    report, do not promote.
  - Confirmation of B: configs 1 AND 2 exceed threshold. No
    angle-branch control is available for B in this design (declared
    asymmetry relative to C -- see protocol Sec. 3).
Configurations, angles, and target n_mod are fixed exactly as
tabulated in protocolo_discriminante_CxB_v1.md, Section 2.

A0-v1 DECISION RULE (locked)
------------------------------
3x3 matrix (orientations 1/2/3 x targets n=4/28/40). Significance via
a SINGLE joint permutation calibration across the full 9-cell pattern
-- not 9 independent per-cell tests at nominal alpha.
  - A0 confirmed:      diagonal pattern (each orientation significant
                        ONLY at its own predicted target).
  - A0-null confirmed:  n=28 significant in ALL three orientations,
                        not at 4 or 40.
  - QM pure:             no significant cell anywhere.
  - "A0-null confirmed" also confirms C, but ONLY in the operational
    sense defined by this protocol (existence of modulation in these
    three specific configurations) -- NOT the full harmonic model.
    E3 and E4 remain untested by this protocol.
Orientations and target n_mod fixed exactly as tabulated in
axioma_0_identificabilidade_v1.md, Sections 3-5. Orientation 2 of
this protocol IS configuration 3 (7:4 D3-exato, ramo 1) of CxB-v1 --
shared, not duplicated, if both protocols run in the same session.
